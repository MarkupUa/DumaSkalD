About DumaSkalD

Ancient memory. Cold earth.
Dark river. Ritual sound.

DumaSkalD brings Ukrainian poetic heritage into the cold, ceremonial world of dark ritual folk. Deep percussion, ancestral memory and ritual voices shape a sound inspired by earth, stone, river and storm.

01 / A living memory

Rooted in poetry.
Carried by sound.

Ukrainian poetry holds the echoes of a landscape: the weight of the earth, the movement of rivers, the persistence of memory. DumaSkalD brings these voices into a contemporary musical language — spare, physical and deeply atmospheric.

The energy of Nordic ritual folk becomes a setting for Ukrainian poetic tradition. Earth, river, wind and stone give this music its vocabulary; historical memory gives it weight. Each composition is an encounter between inherited words and a present-day voice.

02 / DumaSkalD

The shape of the sound

Massive frame drums and deep ritual percussion meet bowed lyre, tagelharpa textures and dark drones. Distant horns and ancestral chants leave space for restrained Ukrainian vocal recitative. The words remain at the centre of the sound.
